# AI Usage Declaration

OpenAI Codex assisted with requirements interpretation, implementation, review, debugging, testing and documentation. Development tools and runtime planning models are distinct; project API ledgers do not measure Codex token usage or costs.

Runtime planning can use the school platform's DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash or DeepSeek-V4-Pro deployments. The planner receives data summaries and validation feedback, chooses executable candidates and may propose bounded parameter or feature changes. It does not receive test scores or execute generated code. Runs with the LLM disabled or unavailable are not evidence of successful LLM planning.

Registered off-peak accounting rates per million tokens are CNY 1 input / 4 output for Flash, and CNY 4.5 input / 13.5 output for Pro. All input uses the cache-miss rate. These calculated costs are not school-platform invoices; the course must confirm the accepted accounting convention. Registries and hash-checked original evidence are under `configs/`.

Each run retains actual returned usage, requests/responses, failures and unresolved reservations in usage.jsonl, model_calls/, model_usage.json and summary.json. Unknown usage is not zero. The two historical Flash timeouts were reconciled on 2026-11-23 at their reserved upper bound (CNY 5.24288 each) with actual usage unrecoverable, so their per-task accounting remains incomplete; provisional development results are not accounting-complete formal submissions. A successful prediction file does not establish complete accounting.

The two reported experiments are now `coupon-minilm2-20261123` (11 recorded Flash calls, CNY 0.059672, held-out 0.791486) and `headlines-minilm2-20261123` (11 recorded Flash calls, CNY 0.076654, held-out 0.670483), both from the scheme that won the 2026-11-24 comparison: Flash planner, 12 trials, MiniLM and feature-search enabled, three-seed validation. The earlier reported pair `coupon-gpu-20261123` (CNY 0.014231, 0.790698) and `headlines-gpu-20261123` (CNY 0.056792, 0.660582) remain valid runs and are kept as the cheaper equivalents; the reported pair costs CNY 0.136326 together. Four search-effort rounds (`coupon-t10` 0.058049, `coupon-t14r` 0.065004, `headlines-t15` 0.074308, `headlines-t20r` 0.089579), two interrupted attempts (`coupon-t14` 0.074266, `headlines-t20` 0.031994) and three superseded interim runs (`coupon-v2` 0.018845, `coupon-v3` 0.019258, `headlines-v2` 0.026555) keep their charges in the ledger. Earlier exploratory runs were removed from local `runs/` on 2026-11-23 and are no longer listed as experiments; their original request/response evidence was copied into `.model_usage/<ledger>/evidence/`, and their recorded charges together with the two conservative timeout reconciliations stay in the ledger as disclosed historical AI usage, not attributed to the reported experiments.

An accuracy-enrichment probe on the same runtime added four more ledgers on the same day: `coupon-minilm2-20261123` (11 calls, CNY 0.059672), `headlines-minilm2-20261123` (11 calls, CNY 0.076654), `headlines-minilm3-20261123` (11 calls, CNY 0.067790) and the aborted `headlines-minilm-20261123` (8 calls, CNY 0.049710), CNY 0.253826 in total. The probe enabled the MiniLM and feature-search capabilities and scored each trial as the mean of three training seeds. Its two winning runs became the reported experiments after the 2026-11-24 scheme comparison; its paired seed-7 control (`headlines-minilm3`) returns exactly 0.660582 against the reported 0.670483, which is disclosed as seed variance rather than a systematic gain.

A second probe implemented a five-seed final-fit ensemble (`final_fit_seeds`, commit `b8d38fb` on a separate runtime hash) and ran two more tasks: `coupon-ens-20261123` (CNY 0.025366) and `headlines-ens-20261123` (CNY 0.057801), CNY 0.083167. Averaging five refits returned 0.787544 on coupon where the best single seed reaches 0.790698, and reproduced 0.660582 exactly on headlines because all five refits are identical; it removes seed luck instead of adding accuracy. The change was reverted, so the reported experiments remain reproducible at the original runtime hash.

A planner-model swap added `coupon-pro-20261123` (DeepSeek-V4-Pro, CNY 0.122270) and `headlines-pro2-20261123` (DeepSeek-V4-Pro, CNY 0.454991), CNY 0.577261. Pro is priced from official evidence (`configs/model_registry.pro.json`, CNY 4.5 / 13.5 per million) and reproduced the Flash held-out scores exactly, at roughly eight times the planning cost. Qwen was not run: the platform only offers the rolling `Qwen` alias and `Qwen3.8-27B`, released after the 2026-10-21 course cutoff, and no published price covers the 27B alias, so a Qwen ledger could not be priced. A `headlines-pro-20261123` attempt failed before any call on a registry path and carries no charge. With all probes, 2026-11-23 GPU usage is CNY 1.446304 across twenty runs, all accounting complete.
